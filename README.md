# Wild Thistle Moon

Wild Thistle Moon is a micro SaaS concept for content creators who need AI-accelerated planning across Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Pinterest, and YouTube. The Social Constellation Studio translates a single campaign brief into platform-aware copy, SEO/GEO signals, AI asset prompts, and an adaptive publishing calendar.

## Features
- 🎯 **Omnichannel planner** – Capture campaign topic, persona, tone, CTA, cadence, and launch timing, then generate channel-specific copy blueprints with keyword and geo reinforcement.
- 🗓️ **Automated schedule orbit** – Instantly map the best posting windows per platform according to the desired cadence with a ready-to-export grid.
- 🖼️ **AI asset prompts** – Receive Midjourney/SDXL-friendly image prompts, video generation guidance, and accessibility alt-text starters tuned for each network.
- 🤖 **Automation blueprints** – Use built-in LLM, image, and video production workflows to hand off tasks to agents or creative tools without additional prompt engineering.

## Usage
1. Open `index.html` in your browser.
2. Complete the campaign form with your audience insights, objectives, and preferred cadence.
3. Select the platforms you plan to activate and click **Generate omnichannel plan**.
4. Review the tailored copy kits, hashtags, asset prompts, and the suggested schedule. Export or copy the automation blueprints into your tool stack.

Wild Thistle Moon is designed as an inspiration prototype—wire it into your schedulers, DAM, or agent network to bring the constellation to production.
